Project context: Strategic redesign initiative to address observed decline in Uber airport bookings through context-aware design solutions.
01 · Context

The airport booking challenge

Airports represent a unique and high-value segment for ride-sharing services, characterized by predictable demand patterns, higher average trip values, and captive user audiences. However, the product team observed a significant and persistent decline in Uber bookings at airports compared to urban and suburban locations—threatening to erode a valuable market segment.

The airport environment presents a perfect storm of complexity: users navigate unfamiliar terminals while managing luggage, group coordination, and time-sensitive flight schedules—all while trying to secure reliable ground transportation.

02 · Research approach

Understanding the airport user journey

METHODS

Mixed-methods research

Combined quantitative booking data analysis with qualitative diary studies and contextual inquiries at 3 major international airports.

PARTICIPANTS

147 airport riders

Diverse user segments including business travelers, families, solo tourists, and international visitors across different terminals and airlines.

DATA SOURCES

Booking analytics

Analysis of 2.1M airport booking sessions vs. 8.4M non-airport sessions over 18 months.

03 · Key findings

Friction points in the airport experience

Through thematic analysis of user feedback and behavioral data, three primary friction areas emerged that significantly impacted the booking-to-completion conversion funnel:

FINDING 01

Navigation uncertainty dominates pre-trip anxiety

68% of users reported anxiety about finding their Uber at airports, with 42% abandoning booking attempts to seek alternative transportation they perceived as more predictable (taxi queues, hotel shuttles, or public transit).

FINDING 02

Pricing confusion creates trust barriers

Airport surge pricing averaged 2.3x baseline rates during peak arrival/departure windows, yet users lacked visibility into pricing patterns or alternatives, leading to 31% of initiated bookings being cancelled upon price reveal.

FINDING 03

Zone complexity increases cognitive load

Users struggled with airport-specific pickup zone designations (e.g., "Zone 5A", "Cell Phone Lot", "Terminal B Departures"), resulting in 27% of successful bookings requiring rider-driver reconnection attempts after initial mismatch.

04 · Decision 1

Redesign the pre-booking experience for airport contexts

Recognizing that airport users operate under heightened time pressure and cognitive load, I proposed a context-aware booking flow that proactively addresses airport-specific anxieties before commitment:

The solution introduces an "Airport Mode" that activates when GPS detects proximity to major airports, offering:

FEATURE 01

Real-time pickup zone guidance

Visual terminal maps showing exact pickup locations and walking routes

FEATURE 02

Predictive pricing windows

Historical surge patterns and pricing forecasts for better expectation setting

FEATURE 03

Luggage-specific vehicle options

SUV/Van prioritization for travelers with bags

FEATURE 04

Flight integration

Timing coordination based on flight schedules and potential delays

"Rather than overwhelming users with options, the interface surfaces only airport-relevant information at the moment it becomes actionable—reducing cognitive load while increasing confidence in the booking decision."Contextual awareness

08 · Qualitative insights

User response to prototype testing

Usability testing of high-fidelity prototypes with 32 airport travelers revealed strong positive responses to the contextual approach:

"Finally, an app that understands I'm not just requesting a ride—I'm trying to make my flight while dragging three bags through an unfamiliar terminal."

INSIGHT 01

Decreased anxiety

87% of test users reported decreased anxiety about airport pickups

INSIGHT 02

Predictive pricing confidence

76% said the predictive pricing information made them more likely to book rather than seek alternatives

INSIGHT 03

Preferred navigation

69% preferred the step-by-step navigation over traditional map pins for airport environments

INSIGHT 04

Business traveler value

Frequent business travelers specifically valued the flight integration timing features

09 · Reflection

Designing for constrained contexts

The airport use case reinforced a critical principle in transportation UX: when users are operating under time pressure, cognitive load, and physical constraints (luggage, travel fatigue), the interface must reduce decision complexity rather than add features. The most impactful solutions weren't new capabilities, but better timing and presentation of existing information.

This project highlighted how context-aware design can transform high-stress moments into opportunities for building user trust and loyalty—particularly valuable in competitive markets where switching costs are low.
