Case study 02 · Enterprise · Agentic UX

Next-Gen Agentic Customer Support Platform

Replacing fragmented legacy workflows with four interconnected, explainable-AI portals for 25,000+ support staff across India and global markets.

My Role
Lead UX Designer
Scale
25,000+ staff
Timeline
6 months, phase-wise
Key outcome
60–80% time saved
01

The problem: four sources of operational friction

Agents, field engineers, and legal staff were absorbing the cognitive load a system should have carried. Research and shadowing surfaced four concrete bottlenecks:

01

High cognitive load

Agents cross-referenced 4–5 disconnected portals per single ticket, hunting for purchase history and telemetry data by hand.

02

Slow hardware triage

Line-On-Display (LOD) screen cases took 10–15 minutes per device to photograph via third-party camera apps and compile into manual PDF reports.

03

Image quality rejections

15–20% of daily hardware cases were rejected instantly for motion blur or glare — restarting a multi-day repair cycle each time.

04

Paper-heavy legal CCM

Manual data entry from physical court notices, paper courier tracking for Vakalatnama forms, and disconnected communication with external lawyers.

02

The paradigm shift: human-in-the-loop, not autonomous

The core UX decision underneath all four portals: AI never acts alone. It synthesizes context and calculates a confidence score, presents it through a generative UI with evidence attached, and a human verifies and executes with one click.

    03

    Four interconnected portals

    One master AI orchestrator, four role-specific interfaces — each tailored to how that person actually works.

    Field Engineers & SAW Inspection Agents

    Nirnay — AI-Guided Hardware Triage

    Real-time on-device camera guidance with computer vision feedback, blocking blurred uploads before submission and routing verified defects automatically.

    • On-device real-time camera gate — spatial framing guidance to eliminate glare
    • Pre-submission AI check blocks blurry images at point of capture
    • Confidence-based auto-routing: high confidence auto-approves, low confidence triggers 1-click human review
    VOC Support Representatives

    Priority Desk — One-Click VOC Resolution

    Consolidates device history, chat logs, and sentiment analysis into a single timeline — replacing what used to require gathering details across 5 separate tabs.

    • Predictive recommendations with confidence percentage and supporting evidence
    • Auto-generated communication drafts (SMS/email) ready for one-click send
    Measured pilot impact: 60–80% reduction in VOC comprehension time, +112% SLA case closure rate.
    Legal Employee Reps & External Counsel

    Sahay — Legal Case Management

    Modernized paper-heavy legal dispute operations via government e-Jagruti API integration and automated OCR notice ingestion.

    • OCR scans physical legal notices into digital records in seconds
    • AI legal risk sensing recommends Contest vs. Settle/Dismiss from historical rulings
    • 1-click Vakalatnama authorization & courier dispatch routing
    • Smart Next-Date-of-Hearing briefing calendar for external counsel
    SSO Command Center for 25,000+ Staff

    CS Agentic Hub — Master Orchestrator

    The central sign-on gateway and orchestrator. Staff construct custom UI workspaces on the fly using natural-language prompts, without leaving their command center.

    • Daily Smart AI Planner splits workload into autonomous background tasks vs. items needing human approval
    • Generative UI renders live widgets from prompts like "show pending LOD approvals" or "render my CSAT summary"

    05

    Research insights & explainable AI trust

    INSIGHT 01

    Fatigued gatherers, not slow decision-makers

    Shadowing showed agents spent 70% of handling time just searching portals for context — so the fix was evidence-backed summary cards, not a better search tool.

    INSIGHT 02

    AI trust is fragile

    Agents ignored suggestions they couldn't see the reasoning behind. Every recommendation now shows an explicit confidence score, e.g. "94% confidence, based on 1,200 past tickets."

    INSIGHT 03

    One rigid UI fails every role

    Engineers need fast photo flows; lawyers need court briefings. Generative UI lets each role reshape their own workspace instead of forcing one layout on everyone.

    That second insight became a routing rule applied across every portal:

    AUTO / 1-CLICK
    High confidence (85–100%)

    Recommendation is pre-drafted from complete telemetry; agent executes with a single verification click.

    IN-REVIEW
    Low confidence (<85%)

    System flags missing data or discrepancies and routes directly to a human agent for manual verification.

    ON-DEMAND UI
    Generative workspace

    Natural-language prompts render custom widgets on demand, so agents query history without leaving their hub.

    06

    Takeaway

    Every one of these portals solves the same underlying problem in a different costume: people were doing a machine's job of gathering evidence, and a machine was being trusted to do a human's job of deciding. Splitting those two apart — AI gathers and proposes, a human decides and executes — is what made 25,000 people trust a system they'd never fully hand control to.
