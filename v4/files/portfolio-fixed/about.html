About · The person behind the work

I care about how the whole product makes people feel—not only how a screen looks.

My practice connects research, systems thinking, and interaction design. I’m most interested in work where the problem is complex, the constraints are real, and thoughtful design can make the next step clearer.

01 / Perspective

From computer science to product systems.

I studied Computer Science at IIIT Vadodara, which turned out to be the right foundation for design systems work—thinking in components, states, and rules rather than one-off screens.

Four years at Onsitego taught me that a design system isn’t a file; it’s an argument you have to keep winning across teams. Now at Samsung R&D Institute India, I’m applying that same systems thinking to AI products, where the interface has to represent something that reasons and acts, not just displays.

I care about the parts of a product people notice when they’re missing: the spacing that makes a screen calm, the state that helps an AI feel trustworthy, and the shared rules that keep teams aligned.
