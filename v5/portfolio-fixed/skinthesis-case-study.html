Skinthesis

A research-led exploration into safer, more personalized skincare in India — connecting market evidence, user behaviour, professional guidance and product opportunity.

Overview

Skinthesis started as an exploration of India's fast-growing skincare and wellness landscape. The research progressively narrowed the opportunity toward a more specific problem: people want healthier skin, but often lack confidence in product selection, diagnosis, routine-building, and follow-up care.

03

What the research converged on

  • Lack of personal knowledge and confidence — knowing skincare matters isn't the same as knowing how to interpret ingredients or build a routine
  • Lack of personalized attention — a gap between generic recommendations and advice that accounts for individual skin, lifestyle and concerns
  • Misleading product suitability claims — labels like "for dry skin" don't reliably translate into a confident decision
  • Trial-and-error behaviour — low confidence leads to inconsistent routines shaped by social content and word of mouth
  • Difficulty sustaining care — even a chosen routine needs reminders, follow-up and a way to adapt over time

The opportunity isn't simply "personalized recommendations." It's a system that helps people make, understand, follow and revisit safer skincare decisions.

04

Persona

Representative user story

"I want to understand my skin before I spend money on products."

Young, urban, research-oriented skincare consumer

Aware of skincare and researches before purchasing, but still relies on trial-and-error when confidence runs out. Values sunscreen and professional advice, and wants personalized recommendations rather than sales-led guidance.

05

From problem to product system

Understand

Skin profile, concerns, lifestyle, stress, sleep, preferences and constraints.

Decide

Ingredient analysis, suitability verification, product recommendations and professional consultation.

Act

Personalized routines, reminders, hydration tracking, product usage tracking.

Learn

Natural skincare education, preventive-care content, community experiences, myth reduction.

Connect

Certified dermatologist access, appointment scheduling and regular follow-ups.

Adapt

Feedback loops that let the routine evolve with the user's skin and lifestyle.

06

Feature concepts

  • Tiered subscription plans — professional and personalized care at multiple budget levels, instead of one service model for everyone
  • Personalized skincare routine — generated around skin type, concerns and lifestyle, with customizable steps and frequency
  • Product suitability verification — scan a barcode or QR code to surface ingredients, intended skin type, and potential concerns
  • Virtual dermatologist consultation — connects users to professionals when the problem needs expertise, not another product
  • Progress, product & hydration tracking — lightweight reminders and tracking to sustain routines over time
07

Reflection

The central experience principle became confidence — confidence in understanding one's skin, choosing products, following a routine, and knowing when to seek professional help. Next steps would include source verification for every market claim, stronger clinical guardrails, dermatologist validation, accessibility testing, and a sharper MVP focused on suitability + routine + professional escalation.

Process

From evidence to product direction

02

03

06

Deliver

Document decisions, states, components, and handoff details so the work can move into implementation.

Outcome

A focused skincare product direction built around confidence, suitability, routines, and professional escalation. The work converted broad market exploration into a coherent product opportunity and a prioritized feature system.
